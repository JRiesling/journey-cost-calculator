Find parking at your destination

Search for car parks near where you're heading — then add your chosen spot to your route and export everything to your map app.

Enter your destination above to find nearby car parks.

How it works

FuelSmarter searches for car parks near your destination using Google Maps data. JustPark results appear first — these are bookable in advance so your space is guaranteed. Google Maps results fill in where JustPark doesn't have listings.

Add parking to your route

Once you've chosen a car park, tap "Add to route" and it'll appear in your journey bar alongside your fuel stop and service station stops. Hit "Export to Maps" and your whole journey opens in your map app — ready to go.
