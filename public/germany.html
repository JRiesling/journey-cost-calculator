Tips for UK drivers in Germany

🚗 Keep right except to overtake. This is strictly enforced in Germany. Undertaking (passing on the right) is illegal and dangerous — very different to UK motorway habits.

⛽ Fill up away from Autobahn service stations. Fuel at Autobahn Raststätten is 8-15 cents per litre more expensive than at town filling stations. Plan stops accordingly.

🔍 Watch your mirrors constantly. On unrestricted sections cars can approach at 200+ km/h. Always check before moving lanes.

📋 Carry essential documents. UK licence, V5C, insurance certificate, GB sticker, warning triangle, and hi-vis vest are all required.

💶 No motorway tolls for cars. Unlike France, Germany charges no tolls for private cars on the Autobahn — only HGVs pay. A major advantage for road trippers.

Frequently asked questions

Is there really no speed limit on the German Autobahn?
Around 70% of the Autobahn has no general speed limit, with an advisory speed (Richtgeschwindigkeit) of 130 km/h. The remaining 30% has permanent or variable speed limits, typically 120-130 km/h. Even on unrestricted sections, exceeding the advisory speed can affect liability in an accident.
Are there tolls on German motorways?
No — German Autobahns are free for private cars and motorcycles. Only HGVs (trucks over 7.5 tonnes) pay motorway tolls in Germany. This is a major advantage over France, where autoroute tolls can add €50-100 to a long journey.
Is fuel cheaper in Germany than the UK?
Yes — both petrol and diesel are cheaper in Germany. Diesel is significantly cheaper — around 44p per litre less than in the UK in May 2026. If you're driving a diesel car, filling up in Germany rather than the UK before crossing the Channel can save meaningful amounts.
How does driving at Autobahn speeds affect fuel consumption?
Significantly. Driving at 180 km/h uses around 28% more fuel than driving at 130 km/h due to aerodynamic drag. On a 500km journey this adds around €10 in fuel costs. Use our calculator set to Germany to see the exact impact for your car and speed.
