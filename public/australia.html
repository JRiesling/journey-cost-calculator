The weekly petrol price cycle — Australia's unique fuel market

Australia has one of the world's most distinctive fuel pricing systems. In the major east coast cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane), petrol prices follow a predictable weekly cycle — rising sharply at the start of the week, then falling steadily until they bottom out mid-week before rising again.

⏰ Best days to fill up in Australia

Tuesday to Thursday — prices are typically at their lowest mid-week. Filling up on a Thursday instead of a Sunday can save up to 15 cents per litre, or around $8 on a 55-litre tank. In Perth, FuelWatch regulates the cycle, with prices rising on Wednesday mornings — fill up on Tuesday in Perth.

The cycle isn't perfectly consistent — some weeks run 7 days, others stretch to 10-14 days. The best approach is to check a price comparison app before filling up rather than assuming a specific day will always be cheapest.

💡 Tips to save money on petrol in Australia

📱 Use GasBuddy or MotorMouth — Australia's best apps for finding the cheapest petrol near you, with prices updated by the community.
🏪 Woolworths and Coles fuel discounts — both supermarket chains offer 4 cents per litre off fuel with their loyalty cards. Stack with shopper dockets for bigger savings.
📅 Fill up Tuesday to Thursday — prices are consistently lowest mid-week in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane due to the weekly price cycle.
🚐 Plan fuel stops on outback routes — remote roadhouses charge a significant premium. Carry a jerry can for long outback legs where stations are scarce.
🚗 Check tyre pressure — Australian roads can be long and hot. Under-inflated tyres increase fuel consumption and blowout risk, particularly in summer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average petrol price in Australia in 2026?

Around 205 cents per litre (c/L) nationally, with Adelaide the cheapest capital at 193 c/L and Darwin the most expensive at 215 c/L. Remote areas can be significantly higher.

What is the cheapest day to buy petrol in Australia?

Tuesday to Thursday in the major east coast cities, where the weekly price cycle bottoms out mid-week. In Perth, fill up on Tuesday before Wednesday morning when FuelWatch allows prices to rise.

Why is petrol so expensive in remote Australia?

Remote roadhouses face much higher distribution and transport costs — fuel has to be trucked hundreds of kilometres from the nearest terminal. Lower competition and higher operating costs also contribute. Expect to pay 50-100 c/L more in outback areas than in capital cities.

Does Australia use litres or gallons for fuel?

Litres — all Australian fuel is priced and sold in litres. Fuel economy is typically expressed as litres per 100 kilometres (L/100km) rather than miles per gallon. Our calculator supports both measurement systems.
