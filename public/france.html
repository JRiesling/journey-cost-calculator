🇫🇷 Driving in France — Fuel Costs & Road Trip Guide

Current French petrol and diesel prices, motorway toll costs, speed limits, and everything UK drivers need to know before crossing the Channel.

French fuel prices right now

Petrol (Sans Plomb 95) in France currently costs around €1.92 per litre — significantly more than the EU average of €1.54/L, ranking France among the more expensive EU countries for fuel. Diesel sits at around €1.95/L. Prices have risen sharply in 2026 following geopolitical tensions affecting global oil supply.

French motorway tolls — what to expect

Unlike the UK, most French motorways (autoroutes) charge tolls. These can add significantly to the cost of a French road trip. Here are some example toll costs for a standard car:

RouteApproximate toll cost
Calais to Paris (A26/A1)€25–€30
Paris to Lyon (A6)€30–€35
Paris to Nice (A6/A7/A8)€70–€85
Calais to Bordeaux€55–€65

You can pay French tolls with a credit card at most toll booths. Consider getting a Liber-t transponder for faster passage if you drive in France regularly.

⚠️ Important for UK drivers in France

Since Brexit, UK driving licences are still valid in France. You must carry your V5C logbook, insurance certificate, and a GB sticker or number plate. Breathalysers are no longer legally required but remain recommended. Speed cameras are very common — France had over 4,500 fixed cameras as of 2026.

💡 Tips to save money on fuel in France

🛒 Fill up at supermarkets — Leclerc, Intermarché, and Carrefour stations are consistently 8–12 cents per litre cheaper than motorway services.
🛣️ Avoid autoroute fuel stations — motorway services in France are among the most expensive in Europe. Fill up before joining the autoroute.
🗺️ Use Waze or ViaMichelin — both show current fuel prices at stations along your route in France.
📅 Fill up in November — French petrol prices are historically cheapest in November, most expensive in April. Plan long trips accordingly.
🇱🇺 Fill up in Luxembourg — if your route passes through Luxembourg, fuel is up to 27% cheaper there than in France.

Frequently asked questions

How much does petrol cost in France in 2026?

Around €1.92 per litre for SP95 petrol and €1.95/L for diesel as of July 2026. France is above the EU average for fuel prices due to high taxation.

Do I need to pay motorway tolls in France?

Yes — most French motorways (autoroutes) charge tolls. A drive from Calais to Paris costs around €25–€30 in tolls alone. You can pay by card at most toll booths.

What speed can I drive on French motorways?

130 km/h (81 mph) in dry conditions, reduced to 110 km/h (68 mph) in wet weather. Speed cameras are widespread and fines can be issued to UK drivers.

Where is the cheapest fuel in France?

Supermarket forecourts — particularly Leclerc and Intermarché — are consistently the cheapest. Motorway service stations are the most expensive. Neighbouring Luxembourg has significantly cheaper fuel if your route allows a detour.
