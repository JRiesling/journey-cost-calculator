Most Fuel Efficient Small Cars UK 2026

Real-world MPG figures, running costs, and our top picks for city cars and superminis that go furthest on a tank.

Updated June 2026  ·  8 min read

With petrol sitting at around £1.44 per litre in 2026, a small car that returns 65mpg versus 40mpg can save you over £500 a year — and that's before you factor in lower road tax and insurance. Small cars have always led the way on fuel economy, and the latest crop of hybrids and efficient petrols make the case even stronger.

This guide focuses on conventional petrol, diesel, and self-charging hybrid small cars. We've left out plug-in hybrids, since their headline MPG figures are largely a product of the WLTP test rather than real-world driving.

Top 5 most fuel efficient small cars

#1 Pick

Toyota Yaris Hybrid

Up to 70.6mpg official · 55–65mpg real-world

The Yaris Hybrid has been the benchmark for small car efficiency for years, and the current generation cements that status. Toyota's self-charging hybrid system is at its best in exactly the conditions small car buyers spend most of their time in — town driving, school runs, and stop-start commutes.

Real-world owners consistently report 55–65mpg in mixed driving, with urban-heavy drivers seeing figures nudging 70mpg. It's also one of the most reliable cars on sale, with Toyota's hybrid system requiring minimal maintenance over high mileage.

Tips to maximise your MPG

  • Maintain tyre pressure: Under-inflated tyres increase rolling resistance and can cost you 2–3mpg.
  • Accelerate smoothly: Harsh acceleration burns significantly more fuel. Gentle throttle inputs let the hybrid system prioritise electric power for longer.
  • Remove unnecessary weight: An extra 50kg in the boot increases fuel consumption by roughly 1–2%.
  • Use eco mode: Most hybrids have a dedicated eco driving mode that adjusts throttle response to prioritise economy.
  • Plan longer trips: Short journeys from cold hurt economy significantly, as the engine works harder until it reaches operating temperature.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most fuel efficient small car in the UK in 2026?

The Mazda 2 Hybrid has the highest official WLTP figure at 74.3mpg, though it's mechanically identical to the Toyota Yaris Hybrid. In real-world terms, both return 55–68mpg depending on how and where you drive.

Are hybrid small cars worth the extra cost?

For most urban and suburban drivers, yes. The fuel savings of 15–25mpg over a comparable petrol car will typically recover the purchase premium within 3–5 years, and you also benefit from lower road tax.

Is diesel still a good option for a small car?

Diesel is worth considering if you do a high annual mileage on A-roads or motorways. For predominantly urban driving, a self-charging hybrid will be more efficient.
