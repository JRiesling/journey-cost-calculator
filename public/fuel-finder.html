How it works

Fuel Smarter uses the UK Government's official Fuel Finder database — every petrol station in the UK is legally required to update their prices within 30 minutes of any change. We calculate your route, then search for the cheapest stations within 1 mile of your journey.

Why prices vary so much

Supermarket forecourts (Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury's, Morrisons) are consistently 3-8p per litre cheaper than branded stations and motorway services. On a 50-litre fill up that's £1.50-£4 difference — worth planning for on a long journey.
