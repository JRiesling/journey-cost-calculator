How it works

Fuel Smarter uses the UK Government's official Fuel Finder database — every petrol station is legally required to update prices within 30 minutes of any change. We search stations within 1 mile of your location or route.

Why use supermarkets?

Supermarket forecourts (Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury's, Morrisons) are consistently 3-8p/litre cheaper than branded stations and motorway services. On a 50-litre fill up that's up to £4 difference.
